Privacy Policy

36 frames, no previews, and nothing leaves your phone. Here's what you need to know about how this app handles data.

1. No Data Collection

This app does not track you, profile you, or send your photos anywhere. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no servers.

2. No Accounts or Sign-Ups

You don't need to create an account, sign in, or share personal information to use the app.

3. Your Roll Stays on Your Phone

Frames you shoot are kept inside the app on your device until you process the roll. Then they're saved to your Photos library and deleted from the app. The app keeps only the latest contact sheet.

4. Permissions Explained

  • Camera — Needed to shoot your roll.
  • Photos (add only) — Needed to save developed frames and the contact sheet. The app can add photos but can't see your library.
  • Location (optional) — Adds where each frame was shot to the photo's details. Only if you allow it, and it stays in the photo on your device.

5. Purchases

Your first roll is free. More rolls are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

6. Standard App Analytics

We use Apple's built-in App Analytics through the App Store. This data is anonymous, does not identify you personally, and helps us improve the app.

7. Contact

If you have questions, feedback, or notice something off, you can reach me at: verysimplefeedback@gmail.com.

✅ Summary:

  • Your photos stay on your device.
  • We don't track you.
  • Permissions are only used for things you control.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We see no payment details.
